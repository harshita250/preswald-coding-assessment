# 🌿 NYC Green Infrastructure Dashboard

This interactive data app explores how and where Green Infrastructure (GI) is being deployed across New York City using open data from the NYC Department of Environmental Protection (DEP).

---

## 🚀 What This App Shows

- 📍 Where GI projects are concentrated (Queens & Brooklyn lead)
- 🌳 What kinds of GI are being installed (e.g., rain gardens, bioswales)
- 🌲 Tree coverage — boroughs with greener GI projects
- 🌊 Sewer system types — uncovering why some areas are prioritized (more Combined sewer systems = more flood risk)

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- **Queens and Brooklyn** not only have the highest number of GI installations, but they also lead in **tree-integrated designs**
- These boroughs also have the **highest share of Combined sewer systems**, making them more vulnerable to flooding and therefore a focus for stormwater mitigation
- GI deployment reflects both **environmental justice** and **infrastructure strategy**
